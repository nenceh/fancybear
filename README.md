3. <b>Set up</b> your device's camera

- Set a countdown timer for each photo (default: 3 seconds)
- Choose and apply a camera filter

<br/>
4. <b>Capture</b> your photos in a <b>4-cut</b> style

- Time to strike your best poses and have fun!

<br/>
5. <b>Customize</b> and personalize your photo strip

- Now's your chance to edit your photo strip
- Tap to change the
    * 🎨 <b>frame colour</b>,
    * ⭐ <b>add stickers</b>,
    * or ↩️ <b>retake</b> to try again
- Tap the <span style="color: #C3D350;font-weight: bold;padding:0 2px;">I'm done</span> button to get your finished image

## Privacy Policy
Fancy Bear ("Fancy Bear", "we", "us" or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it. We do not use any cookies and did not implement any trackers on this site. We do not track, collect, or store any personal data.

To use our photo booth service, you must enable camera access on your device. We do not have access to your footage. All photos are taken and processed locally on your device. No photos are uploaded or saved to any external server.
